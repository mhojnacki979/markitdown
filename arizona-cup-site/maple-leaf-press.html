Sponsor Spotlight

Maple Leaf Press

Established in 1969

Maple Leaf Press was the first World Archery (formerly FITA) Official Manufacturer of Archery Target Faces and is the only Official United States Producer. They offer a full line of Indoor, Outdoor and World Archery Field Targets. In 1999, Maple Leaf Press became the Exclusive Official NFAA Manufacturer of target faces, adding The World Archery Festival & Vegas Shoot to their Official line in 2000, along with NFAA Field, Hunter, Indoor and IFAA-approved Animal Targets, plus Game, Authentic™ and Lifesize™ Animal Targets.

Learn more at mapleleafpress.com
