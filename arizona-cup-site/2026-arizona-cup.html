Announcement · May 2025

2026 Arizona Cup

The 2025 AAE Arizona Cup marked an incredible milestone — 36 years of competition, community, and unforgettable memories. We are deeply grateful to everyone who has been part of this journey so far.

Unfortunately, ongoing scheduling challenges within the archery calendar have led to the difficult decision not to hold the Arizona Cup in 2026. We sincerely appreciate your understanding and continued support, and we hope to welcome archers back to Phoenix in the future.

A heartfelt thank you goes out to our longtime title sponsor Arizona Archery Enterprises, as well as Maple Leaf Press, American Whitetail, and the Arizona Game & Fish Department for their unwavering support throughout the years.
