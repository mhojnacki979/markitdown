Registration

Registration is currently closed — the 2026 Arizona Cup is on hiatus. Check back for future events, or get notified when registration reopens.

How registration works

Registration for the Arizona Cup is handled through USA Archery's official Sport80 event system. When an event is active, you'll register and pay there directly.

2025 registered archers list

Membership requirements

All USA participants must hold a current USA Archery membership. International archers must be members of a World Archery member association — or purchase a Temporary Membership. See Event Information for full details.
