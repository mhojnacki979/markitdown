Title Sponsor

Arizona Archery Enterprises

The Arizona Cup is proud to present its title sponsor.

AAE continues to be the world's largest manufacturer of nocks and vanes. Whether it is one of their own designs or custom-designed arrow components for one of their archery partners, you can be assured that quality is the most important aspect of the product.

Look for the Elite Plastifletch vanes and Plastinocks, the Plastifletch Max, Max Hunter, Pro Max, and WAV Vanes — plus Fastset Gel and Max Bond Glue with the easy-to-use Fletch III fletching jig.

In 2006, AAE's archery division became AAE Cavalier USA with the purchase of Cavalier USA, continuing the full line of recurve accessories — the Elite Finger Tab, the Free Flyte family of arrow rests, the Champion II rest, the cushion Master Plunger, clickers, and the Pro String Server — alongside compound accessories including the Full Capture Fall-A-Way rests, DOA and Pro Drop, coiled-spring rests like the Hawk Eye, Lil' FreakShow and Stinger, and target rests like the FreakShow and Pro Blade.

Whatever your archery accessory needs, you can count on the quality that over 40 years of experience has brought to Arizona Archery Enterprises.
