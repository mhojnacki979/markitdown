General Information

Event Information

Venue

Ben Avery Shooting Facility — FITA Archery Event Range
37016 North Archery Dr, Phoenix, Arizona, USA

Facility aerial map (PDF)

Membership

USA participants: must be a current member of USA Archery. Check whether your membership expires before the tournament and renew beforehand.

Non-USA participants: must be a current member of another World Archery member association, in good financial standing with World Archery.

Competition rules & format

  • World Archery Rules
  • USA Archery Fixed Pins
  • USA Archery Dress Code (PDF)

Age categories

U18 — through the calendar year of your 17th birthday. U21 — through the calendar year of your 20th birthday. Seniors — any age. 50+ — from the year of your 50th birthday. Para division offered in Recurve and Compound (Senior).

Bow types

Recurve · Barebow · Compound · Fixed Pins

Target faces

Recurve & Barebow — 122 cm face. Compound & Fixed Pins — 80 cm face (6-ring).

Distances

Compound, Fixed Pins & Barebow — 50 m. Recurve U18 & 50+ — 60 m. Recurve U21 & Senior — 70 m.

Qualification round

72 arrows total · 6-arrow ends · 4 minutes per end.

Elimination rounds

Top 64 per division advance to Olympic-round match play. 3 arrows per end, 2 minutes per end. Recurve & Barebow — first to 6 set points. Compound & Fixed Pins — 5 ends, highest cumulative score.
